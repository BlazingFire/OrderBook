# OrderBook 1.4.5

**Draft.** Not released. Release date: to be finalised by the lead session. Items in "Check before release" are not yet
confirmed. Written from the plan and the helpers' logs of the 2026-10-07 batch; wording is checked against each
final commit before release.

## Install order

As before:
1. Install the Master Setup over the existing install first. There is no need to uninstall
   it; the data is kept.
2. Then install each Office Setup, or the Win7 Office Setup on Windows 7.
3. From then on, updates are automatic.

## What changed

- **A new Filling note and Dispatch note.** Both notes are printed on A4 in the new design.
  - The quantity unit is **box**.
  - The coded sub products (numbers such as 102 or 1111) sit in a grid of one code per cell: three columns beside the
    named products, seven on a page that has coded products only. A product's name is kept above its codes, and never cut.
  - A long note is split across pages so the last page is never nearly empty.
  - The fonts are bundled with the program, so printing needs no internet. The month is written as a name ("Oct").
- **Colours in the pending order Enter flow.** When a sub product comes in several colours, the colour is now a step of
  the keyboard flow, on both the Master and the Office.
  - Type or pick a colour; **Enter** adds the highlighted colour and stays in the colour box for the next one.
  - **Enter on the empty box** goes on to the quantities. Several colours of one sub product can be added quickly.
  - A sub product with one fixed colour shows it and is skipped; a numbered sub product has no colour step.
- **Products added or changed appear in an open pending order.** If an administrator (or the Master) adds a product, or
  edits one, while someone is entering a pending order on the Master or on an Office, it shows up in that open screen
  at once, without closing and reopening it.
- **A notice board.** The company can put up short notices that everybody signed in reads on both the Master and the
  Office.
  - A notice has a title, a plain-text message, a priority (normal or important), an optional pin, an optional
    "show until" date, and who it is for: everybody, or the people holding chosen roles.
  - Open **Notice board** in the sidebar (or press Alt+N). Unread notices are marked, and a badge counts them. Important
    and pinned unread notices also appear on the Dashboard. "Mark as read" needs the Master to be reachable.
  - A notice disappears from the board after its last day. Pinned notices come first, then important ones, then the newest.
  - A notice is only text: line breaks are kept, nothing else is shown as formatting or as a link.
  - Each person sees only their own notices. The board still opens when the Master is unreachable, from what that person had
    last time. Adding, editing and "mark as read" wait for the Master.
- **Notice board management.** A separate menu, **Notice board management** (Alt+M), for the people who may manage
  notices. They can add, edit, pin, set an end date, choose the audience, and delete. They see every notice, including
  expired ones, how many people have read each, and who. A deleted notice goes to the Recycle Bin and can be restored.
  - Two people editing the same notice: the second save is refused with a message to reload, so nobody's
    changes are lost silently.
  - In Access, each role has a **Notice board** part: **Read** (every role has it after the update) and **Manage**
    (administrator roles have it after the update; a role below administrator cannot be given it).
- **Product photos.**
  - New on the Master, **Products > Photo import**: choose a folder (or many image files) named by sub product code,
    for example `OPT-0011.jpg`; `OPT-0011_2.jpg` is a second photo. Each photo is attached to its sub product whole, without
    cropping, and a report lists what matched and what did not (no such code, duplicate name, not an image).
    You choose whether sub products that already have a photo are skipped or replaced.
  - **The catalogue photos come with the Master.** The photos from the SWISWARE 2026 catalogue are included with the Master program
    and attach themselves, once, to the sub products that have no photo yet. A photo you already added is never replaced.
- **Catalogue corrections.** The catalogue was read page by page against the SWISWARE 2026 catalogue and corrected,
  on a new install and, by one update step, on an existing one.
  - Page 4 toilets: the 14 One Piece Toilets are now the numbered models `102`, `111`, `103`, ... with the trap size in
    the Size column (`9", L-27, B-15, H-26`); the same for `SYPHONIC (9")`. Numbered models go in the Code column of a note,
    not the Model column.
  - **Old pending orders and orders are corrected too:** their lines are renamed with the sub product, so an old note prints
    the number in the Code column. Nothing is deleted, and every id stays, so orders, photos and party records
    keep pointing at the same sub product.
  - `14x14 NEW CORNER` is a **Table Top Basin**: it moves from Wash Basin to Table Top Wash Basin (new code `TTW-0016`, was
    `WB-0017`) and takes that product's rule (no colour).
  - Page 13: the "all colours" note covers only the trap closets (E.W.C and Anglo Indian S and P trap). The four concealed and
    modern closets above them (Eco Concealed, Eco Concealed Slim, Modern, Concealed) are white only.
  - Confirmed as printed, no change: Silver Vitrosa Double Colour and the other page 25 sets come in just the colours printed;
    the coloured pans on page 26 are colour examples, not models; there is no E-04 or E-05; the page 18 Plain Set 18x14 and the
    page 25 18"x14" Vitrosa Double Colour are different products.
  - A sub product the company has already changed itself is left as it is.
- **The OrderBook icon.** The OrderBook icon is now the icon of both programs everywhere: the window, the taskbar, the
  desktop shortcut, the installer, the program file itself, and the tray, on Windows 10 and 11 and on Windows 7, instead of
  the standard Electron icon.
- **Screens from HD to 4K.** The screens were checked at 1280x720 up to 3840x2160, at the sizes Windows scaling really gives
  a 1080p screen (100 %, 125 %, 150 %) and with the app's own size setting.
  - The title at the top of a page is no longer squeezed to a few letters on a smaller screen or at a large size setting,
    and the command box shortens instead.
  - Wider date columns, table headings and button labels that were cut off, and long names in reports, now fit.
  - Darker-theme text that was too faint was darkened or lightened.
  - Nothing scrolls sideways and no dialog runs off the screen at any of these sizes.

## Check before release

- The audit of the notice board found gaps in the first version; confirm each is closed in the final build: a notice title
  must not show in the Activity log to people who cannot see the notice; a notice sent with a missing or wrong audience must
  be refused with a clear message instead of an error; a reader's notice must not list other roles; the Office must show
  nothing from the notice board on a PC with nobody signed in; a role created without the read right gets it.
- Confirm against the final commits: the exact Enter-flow wording; that the live product update works for both an added and an
  edited product on both panels; that the catalogue photos attach once, only to sub products without a photo, on an update and on a new install; the final list of
  screen fixes.
- A real two-PC pass: one Master, one Office with two users of different roles, one administrator; a real print and PDF of both
  notes through the installed program; the installed icon on a clean PC (including Windows 7).
- The dark theme and the Electron window were not exercised end to end.
