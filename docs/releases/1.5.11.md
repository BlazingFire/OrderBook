# OrderBook 1.5.11

An ordinary update: it installs over 1.5.9 or 1.5.10 and keeps all data. No database change, no reset, nothing
to confirm. Update the Master first, then every Office.

## Fixed

- **Office panels can save orders again.** On 1.5.9 and 1.5.10 an Office answered "Product 1 has no variant" and
  refused every new order, and any edit that added a product. Orders entered on the Master were not affected.
- **The Master starts on macOS and Linux.** It quit at launch, and on a Mac its database could not start. This
  release carries Windows setups only.
