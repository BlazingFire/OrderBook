# OrderBook 1.5.12

An ordinary update: it installs over 1.5.9 or later and keeps all data. No database change, no reset, nothing to
confirm. Update the Master first, then every Office. If **Install now** says it failed on a 1.5.11 Office, wait a
minute and press it again: the download carries on behind it.

## New: Back up and Restore for Products, Orders and Parties

On the Master: **Products > Sub products**, **New Orders** and **Final Orders** (one orders backup covers both), and
**Parties**. Back up saves one zip; Restore shows what it would do before anything is written. A restore adds what is
missing and puts back what changed since the backup, and never deletes. A safety copy is saved first. Products and
parties can also be restored onto another Master. The whole-software backup and the nightly automatic backup are unchanged.

## Fixed

- Two people editing different fields of one party or product no longer overwrite each other, and a second edit made
  before the Office syncs no longer undoes the first.
- Editing an order keeps each line's colour and print side, whatever the product is set to now.
- A refused order stays in the form with the reason, instead of being cleared.
- A product code left blank or already used on an Office follows the same rules as on the Master.
- Making a final order keeps its dealer code, grade text size and note ticks. The note typed at dispatch is kept.
- The company's Grade text size reaches every note. "Order edited" alerts ring for Office edits too.
- Queued order edits show their colours and weights before they sync. A long update download no longer shows as failed.
- Deleted products show their code in the Recycle Bin.
