# OrderBook 1.4.0 - release notes

For the owner and the people who use OrderBook in the shops. Plain words, no jargon.

## What is new

**Party Access, redesigned.** The Party Access screen is now a list of every party. For
each one you see whether it is open to everyone or restricted to certain people, and who
those people are, with their names and whether they can only view or can manage. A strip
along the top counts open and restricted parties; click a count to see only those.

- Search by party name, code, city or the name of a person who has access. Press Esc to
  clear the search.
- Pick a person to see every party that person can reach.
- Click a column heading to sort. The column headings stay in view when you scroll.
- Click a party to edit who can see it. The editor is the same on the Master and on the
  Office, and a person who may only look (not change) gets the same window in read-only form.
- In the editor, type a name to add someone, or use **Add several** to tick many people
  and give them all the same level at once. **Copy access** takes the people from another
  party. Nothing is saved until you press Save; if you try to close with unsaved changes
  the window asks first.
- A person who has been switched off is still shown, marked "Inactive user", so you can
  remove them. If you remove the last person, the party becomes visible to everyone and the
  window says so.
- It fits a 1024 x 768 screen even at a large text size.

**Also in 1.4.0, if you have not installed 1.3.0 before** (1.3.0 was never released; both
come together):

- **Chatroom** and **Printroom** for the Office and Management roles.
- **Lists**: grades, packaging and brands are edited from their own screen (Alt+G).
- **Create product share**: tick products and make a PDF of four products per page with
  photographs and no prices.
- **Faces on the order boards**: who is looking at an order shows on its card.
- Sorting and filters on Parties and Products; filling and dispatch note buttons in order
  lists; a show-password eye on every password box; no menu bar at the top of the windows
  (everything moved to the sidebar and to F1 for help).
- **Updates**: a "Check now" button, a download bar, and the Master can update itself every
  night.
- Stock and inventory are gone from the screens. OrderBook is order management only; your
  existing data is kept.

## How to install

Do the Master first. Do not uninstall anything: your data stays.

1. On the Master PC, close OrderBook Master Panel, then run the new **OrderBook Master
   Panel Setup 1.4.0** installer over the existing installation. Say yes when Windows asks
   for permission.
2. Open the Master and sign in. Check Settings, then Updates: it should say 1.4.0 and
   "Up to date".
3. Then, on each Office PC, run the new **OrderBook Office Panel Setup 1.4.0** the same
   way (the Windows 7 PCs use the Windows 7 build).
4. From then on you do not need installers: the Master looks for new versions by itself
   and the Offices update from the Master.

An Office that is on a different version from its Master shows **Update needed** and waits.
It cannot be used until the versions match; install the update or wait for the Master to
reach the same version. An Office that is ahead of its Master also waits, until the Master
catches up.

## Automatic update at 11 pm

The Master can update itself every night at 11 pm (you can change the time in the
Updates screen). It saves unsaved work, signs everybody out, installs the new version and
starts again, so the Offices can reconnect in the morning without anyone at the Master PC.
Nobody is signed in afterwards: you sign in as usual. Leave the Master PC switched on at
that time.
