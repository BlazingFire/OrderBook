# OrderBook 1.5.10

An ordinary update: it installs over 1.5.9 and keeps all data. No database change, no reset, nothing to
confirm. Office panels on 1.5.9 keep connecting; update them when convenient (Master first, then the Offices).
An Office panel prints notes with its own screens, so it shows the new layout once it is on 1.5.10 itself.

## Filling note and Dispatch note

- **Sorted by grade.** Lines follow the company's Grade list (today UT, S.UT, REJ, FOURTH, BHANGAR, then
  A, B, C). Product lines list every UT line first, then S.UT, and so on. Coded products keep their
  categories in name order and list each category's codes by grade. A grade added or moved in Fields sorts
  where it is placed; a grade not on the list sorts last.
- **Each grade starts its own row** of codes inside a category: the UT codes, then a new row for S.UT, and so on.
- **Half and half.** Product lines and coded products each take half of the sheet (they were 58/42).
- **Full names.** A code's name is never cut with "…": a long name wraps, and its row grows to hold it. Names
  longer than ten characters print a little smaller so fewer of them wrap.
- **Grade text size applies to both halves.** The coded products' grade followed a fixed size; it now follows
  the setting like the product lines do, and a large grade makes its row taller. Prints sent to the
  printroom now use the company's grade size too (they ignored it unless the order had its own).
- **Page one is filled** before lines move to the next page. It used to stop early so the last page held at
  least twelve lines, which could leave half of page one blank.
- Category headings 10 px (were 9.5); the Supervisor's name 18 px (was 10).
- The Dispatch note's toolbar no longer offers "Dealer code on this order": its sheet prints no dealer code.
  The Filling note keeps it.

The pending order PDF (Download PDF) follows the same split, sorting, rows, full names and sizes. It prints
its grades at a fixed size, as before.
