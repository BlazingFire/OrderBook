# OrderBook 1.4.6

Released 2026-10-07. Signed with the release key `release-2026-10-01`. Setup installers only.
Download: <https://github.com/BlazingFire/OrderBook-Releases/releases/tag/v1.4.6>

This release is the new look, plus the notice board on Basic Home and at sign-in,
a New Order screen rebuilt as a grid, pending-order and note printing that matches the notes, and fixes to how colours are kept and shown. The design itself is documented in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

## Install order

As before:
1. Install the Master Setup over the existing install first. There is no need to uninstall
   it; the data is kept.
2. Then install each Office Setup, or the Win7 Office Setup on Windows 7.
3. From then on, updates are automatic.

## What changed

### The new look

Nothing about your data, your orders or how the software works has been taken away; what you see is new, on both panels, in light and in dark.

- **The OrderBook mark.** A new logo, a grid of squares, on the start screen, in the sidebar, in the window, in the taskbar and on the desktop icon.
  - The program icon is the mark on a blue tile for the Office Panel and on a white tile for the Master Panel, in the window, the taskbar, the shortcut, the installer and the program file.
  - On start the mark draws itself once (under a second). With "reduce motion" on in Windows it simply appears.
  - While something loads, the mark's squares light up one after another. Spinners are gone. Progress bars and the thin loading line stay.
- **New icons.** The sidebar uses one set of simple line icons in place of the glossy coloured tiles. The Basic Home tiles are colourful again, each with its own colour (the owner asked for this).
- **New buttons and form fields.** One height for every button, a clear blue ring when you move to a control with the keyboard, field edges that are easier to see, round radio buttons, switches,
  and a short message under a field that says what is wrong and what to do. A button that is working shows the busy mark and a short word ("Saving").
- **New sidebar, top bar, tabs and bottom bar** on both panels. The sidebar groups follow the work and a module you may not use is not shown; it folds to a narrow strip of icons. The bottom bar shows the
  sync state first, then the company, who is signed in and the version.
- **New messages and progress.** Progress bars, small messages at the bottom left (they go after four seconds, three at most), hover hints (a short delay, then a dark bubble), banners for offline and conflict,
  empty-list messages and loading placeholders are redrawn.
- **New tables and lists.** Plain rows without stripes, numbers lined up, small coloured status labels, colour dots beside colour names, a copyable id box, and a history that shows who did what and when.
  - **Stage colours.** A stage now shows the colour set for it in Fields (for example Cancelled in red and Postpone in amber, Dispatched in green), on both panels, instead of a green label for everything finished.
  - **Photos in the Products lists.** The Products lists on both panels begin with a small photo of each product (an empty tile when there is none). On the Office they also show with no connection.
- **New dialogs, menus and the Ctrl+K box.** Dialogs have a plain "Cancel" and one main action; menus, the command box and the side panel on a record look and behave the same everywhere.
  - Using the keyboard in a menu works with the arrow keys, Home and End, and when a dialog or menu closes you are put back on the button that opened it. Screen readers are told what each menu and dialog is.
- **Thin scrollbars** that only change colour, no arrows.
- **Screen sizes.** The look was checked from a small laptop screen up to 4K at the usual Windows scalings, in light and dark.

### The New Order screen

On both the Master and the Office. The screen is called **New Order** everywhere; what it saves is still a pending order, and the save messages say "order".
- The top strip holds **Party** (showing the party's name, code and city; a "Create a new party" row opens the New party form with what you typed already in it, and works from the keyboard), **Source**, **Follow up**
  (with "+1 d", "+3 d" and "+1 wk" buttons, which you can also change with the arrow keys) and **Owner**.
- **The product grid.** Pick a product and all its sub products open at once as rows of a table. Type the quantity in a row, and pick its colour in the row itself. A row you leave empty is not added.
  - For a colour you need twice, **+ Same sub product, another colour** sits once below the table.
  - **Enter** walks down the rows and skips the empty ones; **Escape** walks back up.
- **Added products** are grouped by product, each group with its count in products, with a checklist at the foot of what is still needed before you can save, and **Save order**.
- Everything you used still works the same: the colour step for multicolour products, products you add on the Master appear in an open screen, drafts, offline saving, Ctrl+S, and the check on save for products that were deleted.
  There are no prices or stock figures anywhere, as before.
- Fixed: the caret could jump back to row 1 if you typed just as a product card opened.

### Quantities

A quantity is always in pieces ("pcs") everywhere, including the printed Filling and Dispatch notes, the pending-order PDF, the reports, the dashboard and the activity log; "Box" is only a packaging type (the owner's decision).
In the Dispatches report the column is now **Pieces** and the CSV header is `pieces` (it was `boxCount`); a saved link with `?columns=boxCount` still works.

### Printing

- **The pending-order PDF** now uses the same layout as the notes: named lines on the left, coded sub products three to a line (seven to a line on pages of codes alone), a header strip, and the totals and notes on the last page. Its title is "Pending order".
- **Notes** use the codes-only pages seven across, too. A long note is never cut off, on the notes screen or in the PDF.

### Product photos

- Photo files that went missing are rebuilt at start from the catalogue photos that ship with the Master. A photo the company added is never overwritten.
- A photo that cannot be loaded now shows the empty tile instead of a blur; a missing photo file answers quietly (not found) instead of logging an error; the photo on a product's detail is centred.

### Notices

- **Notices on Basic Home.** A band at the top of Basic Home shows the notices that matter to you (important or pinned, not expired, unread first) one at a time, every six seconds. It stops while the pointer is over it or while you
  are using it, has previous, next and stop buttons, and does not move by itself if "reduce motion" is on. Pressing a notice opens it on the Notice board. It shows nothing when there is nothing to show, and only the notices meant for you.
- **Important notices when you sign in.** Right after you sign in, if there are important notices meant for you that you have not read, a window lists them, newest first. **Mark as read** marks them read (on an Office with no connection it
  is remembered and sent when the connection returns); **Later** closes it and it comes back at the next sign-in. It never shows on the sign-in screen, is not repeated if you only refresh the page, and a notice that arrives while you work does not interrupt you.
- **Nothing of the last person's.** When one person signs out and another signs in on the same PC, the new person never briefly sees the previous person's notices.

### Colours

- **Old orders keep what they were sold as.** Editing an old pending order no longer rewrites the colour or colour text of lines that were already on it, even if that product has since stopped being a multicolour product. Only a **new** line of such a product loses its colour.
- **A colour must be on the company colour list.** A new line's colour is matched to the list (capital letters and spacing aside) and refused with a clear message if it is not on it. Old lines are untouched; an Office's queued edit with an unknown colour is rejected with that message instead of being stored.
- **A renamed colour reads the new way everywhere.** After a colour is renamed in Fields, the new name shows on the new order screen, on the pending-order and order records on both panels, on the Filling and Dispatch notes (preview, print and the Printroom sheet) and in the Dispatches report.
  Two old spellings that now read the same count as one row.
- **Dispatches report:** lines with no colour are one "No colour" row instead of a blank.
- **Notes:** the coded sub products on the Filling and Dispatch notes now run **three to a line** (seven to a line on a page of codes alone), so a short list no longer prints one code to a line. Preview, print and PDF are the same.
- A removed Fields value now reports the real moment it was removed.

## What was checked and what was not

Checked on the release candidate:
- The full gate: lint, typecheck and the whole test suite (5,089 tests), and the build.
- Behaviour, by hand, on a live Master with two Offices (the D10 pass, all passed): pairing, notices (board, Basic Home band, sign-in window), New Order on both panels, an Office saving offline and sending when the Master returns, and the colour chain from entry to the notes and the Dispatches report.
- Looks, screen by screen (the D5 pass, all passed): both panels live in the browser at 1366, 1920 and 3840 px, at 100 % and 157 %, in light and dark: no old look left, no sideways scrolling.
- An upgrade from 1.4.5 with real data: no data changed, all photos kept.

Known and left for later: on a 1366 px screen at the largest size, the "API keys" and "Notice board management" titles are shortened, and the Recycle bin's "Pending Order" label is cut.

Not checked:
- A real two-PC pass with the installed program.
- A real print or PDF of both notes and of the pending order from the installed program.
- The installed icon on a clean PC, including Windows 7.
- The Electron window end to end.
