# OrderBook 1.4.6

**Draft.** Not released. Release date: to be finalised by the lead session. Written from the owner's design and the plan
(`CLAUDE_HELPER_UI.md`); each item is checked against the final commits before release (see "Check before release").
The design itself is documented in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

## Install order

As before:
1. Install the Master Setup over the existing install first. There is no need to uninstall
   it; the data is kept.
2. Then install each Office Setup, or the Win7 Office Setup on Windows 7.
3. From then on, updates are automatic.

## What changed

This release is the new look. Nothing about your data, your orders or how the software works has changed; what you see has.

- **The OrderBook mark.** A new logo: a grid of squares, in the program, on the sign-in and start screens, in the window, in the
  taskbar and on the desktop icon. The program names itself underneath: Office Panel or Master Panel.
  - On start, the mark draws itself once (under a second). With "reduce motion" turned on in Windows it simply appears.
  - While something is loading, the mark's squares light up one after another. That is now the only loading signal; the spinners are gone.
- **New icons.** The sidebar and the Basic Home tiles use one consistent set of simple line icons in place of the glossy coloured tiles.
- **New buttons and form fields.** Buttons, text boxes, lists, date boxes, tick boxes and switches follow the new design: the same
  height everywhere, a clear blue ring when you move to one with the keyboard, and a short message under a field that says what is wrong
  and what to do. Buttons use plain verbs ("Save changes", "Convert to order"), and a button that is working shows the busy mark and
  a short word ("Saving").
- **New sidebar, top bar, tabs and bottom bar** on both panels. The sidebar groups follow the work (Work, Catalogue, Output, Admin) and a
  module you may not use is not shown. It folds to a narrow strip of icons. The bottom bar shows whether you are synced, the company,
  who is signed in and the version.
- **New messages and progress.** Progress bars, small messages at the bottom left (they go after four seconds, three at most), hints on
  hover, banners for "offline" and "conflict", empty-list messages and loading placeholders are redrawn.
- **New tables and lists.** Plain rows without stripes, numbers lined up, status in small coloured labels, colour dots beside colour
  names, and a history that shows who did what and when.
- **New dialogs and menus.** Dialogs, the right-click and "more" menus, the Ctrl+K command box and the side panel that opens on a record.
- **Thin scrollbars.** Slim bars that only change colour, no arrows.
- **The new pending order screen**, on both the Master and the Office.
  - The top strip holds Party, Source, Follow up (with "+1 d", "+3 d", "+1 wk" quick buttons) and Owner.
  - The left side is where you add products: search a product, then type colour, quantity, packaging and grade for each sub product.
  - The right side lists the products added so far, with the total, and you can search and remove from it.
  - Everything you used still works the same: Enter moves on, the colour step for multicolour products, products you add on the Master
    appear in an open screen, drafts, offline saving, Ctrl+S, and the check on save for products that were deleted.
  - Quantities are in boxes. There are no prices or stock figures anywhere, as before.
- **A new program icon** in the window, the taskbar, the desktop shortcut, the installer and the program file, with the mark on a blue
  tile (white tile with a blue mark for the Master Panel).
- **Dark theme** follows the same design.
